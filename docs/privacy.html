Blink Buddy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Blink Buddy is a browser extension that reminds you to rest your eyes. It does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and it makes no network requests.

What Blink Buddy stores

Your settings, saved with Chrome's built-in extension storage on your computer: whether breaks are on, whether breaks show over the page, and your chosen character and colour. Blink Buddy's developer never receives them.

Photos

If you use "Make it from a photo", the photo is looked at on your computer: by Chrome's built-in, on-device AI (Gemini Nano) when your Chrome offers it, or by reading the photo's colours when it doesn't. Blink Buddy never uploads or saves the photo; it keeps only the character and colour that were chosen. Chrome's on-device AI is part of Chrome and is covered by Google's own terms.

Access to websites (optional)

Only if you turn on "Show breaks over the page" does Blink Buddy ask for access to websites. It uses that access for one thing: showing the break on the page you're viewing and pausing any video playing there until the break ends. It does not read page content, record your browsing or send anything anywhere. You can turn it off in the popup at any time, which also removes the access.

What Blink Buddy does not do

  • No accounts, analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising.
  • No remote code: everything it runs is inside the extension.
